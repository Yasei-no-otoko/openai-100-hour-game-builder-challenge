01 / SLIDE TO MOVE

Slide on the movement pad in the direction you want to go. Lift your finger to stop. You can also drag on the battlefield. Aiming and firing are automatic.

02 / RETURN FIRE

Tap PARRY as bullets approach. Catch them just after activation for a stronger perfect parry. Keep moving with one finger and parry with the other.

03 / DASH THROUGH

Tap DASH to move briefly unharmed. When stopped, you dash in your last movement direction. Lasers cannot be parried: dash through them.

04 / CLEAR THE SKY

Graze bullets, parry or destroy enemies to charge energy. When full, tap NOVA to erase enemy bullets and lasers. Training gives you unlimited attempts.

05 / KEEP IT. OR BREAK IT.

Tap a card to compare, then confirm below. Pause → Break pact restores 1 hull and clears enemy fire. You lose all pact effects, and enemies attack 25% more often.

06 / YOUR HANDS. YOUR LAYOUT.

Swap the movement pad and action buttons in Settings. Losing focus, hiding the page or rotating the screen pauses the game. Tap Resume when you return.

01 / MOVE & SHOOT

Move with WASD or the arrow keys.
Aim with the mouse; hold left-click to fire.
Hold J to fire with automatic aim.

02 / RETURN FIRE

Press E or right-click to reflect nearby bullets.
The first 0.105 seconds grant a perfect parry.
Return a whole barrage for devastating damage.

03 / SLIP THROUGH

Press SPACE or SHIFT for an invincible dash.
Magenta lasers cannot be parried. Dash through them.

04 / TURN RISK INTO ENERGY

Graze bullets, score kills and parry to gain energy.
Press F for Nova: damage enemies and clear all fire.
Your bullet hitbox is the tiny white dot at your center.

05 / CHOOSE YOUR TERMS

A pact applies to every fight in its sector.
Q breaks it: +1 hull, clear fire, 20% boss damage.
Lose all pact effects. Enemy attack rate rises 25%.

06 / BUILD YOUR ANSWER

After each wave, choose one of three upgrades.
Mix spread + piercing, parry + Nova, or echoes + satellites.
